Ingredients
KOKO KRUNCH
20g honey
20g agave syrup
50g puffed rice
20g oatmeal
20g sunflower seeds
20g sesame seeds
20g blanched almonds
20g dried cranberries
1 egg
4 tbsp canola oil
Preparation
1.
 10min
Preheat the oven to 170 degrees C

2.
 1min
Use a blender to reduce the KOKO KRUNCH® to a fine powder, then add the agave and honey

3.
 1min
In a large bowl combine the puffed rice, oatmeal, sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, cranberries and almonds

4.
 1min
Add the KOKO KRUNCH® and honey mixture

5.
 1min
Add the egg, lightly beaten, and stir to combine, then add the oil

6.
 2min
Tip the mix into a square tin lined with baking parchment, pressing the mix down firmly

7.
 14min
Bake for 10-15 minutes until set

8.
 10min
Leave to cool before carefully lifting out of the tin and portioning into ten equal bars
